SNAKE³

How to play

  • Eat berries to grow. Don't run into your own body.
  • Red berries are worth 100. Rarer blue berries start turning up later and are worth 300.
  • The snake slides over the edges of the cube, and the cube turns to show where you're heading.
  • Once your snake is long, a blinking gold berry sometimes appears for a few seconds. It's worth 1,000 and makes you 5 shorter.
  • A faint berry glowing through the cube is on a side you can't see yet.
  • Every few berries a rock appears. Hitting a rock ends the game too.
  • You speed up a little with every berry.

Touch

  • Swipe left or right, or tap the left or right side of the screen, to turn.
  • Turns are the snake's own left and right, not the screen's.

Keyboard

  • ← → or Q E turn left / right
  • W A S D head up, left, down or right on screen
  • Space pause
